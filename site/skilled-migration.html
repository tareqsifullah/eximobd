Skilled migration

Permanent residence, by points.

For engineers, IT professionals, nurses, accountants and other graduates with experience. We assess your points honestly, plan your language test and skills assessment, and prepare your profile and application.

02What we do

From points check to landing.

  1. Eligibility & points

    We score your age, education, experience and language for each country and show where you stand.

  2. Language plan

    Target scores for IELTS General, CELPIP, PTE Core or TEF — with a partner course if needed.

  3. Credentials & skills assessment

    Educational Credential Assessment (WES and others) or skills assessment with Engineers Australia, ACS or VETASSESS.

  4. Profile & invitation

    Express Entry profile, SkillSelect EOI or state nomination, timed for the best draw.

  5. PR application

    Police certificates, medicals, proof of funds and family documents, organised and checked.

  6. Landing

    Settlement planning: first steps, job search and credential licensing.

03Tip

Your English score can be worth more than a degree.

In Canada’s Express Entry and Australia’s points test, moving from a competent to an expert English score adds more points than most extra qualifications. Our IELTS partner prepares you for the band you need.
