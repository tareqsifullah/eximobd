Germany

Germany's public universities charge little or no tuition for most programmes, and graduates can stay to look for work. English-taught master's programmes are widely available; German helps for jobs.

Main intakes
Apr · Oct
After study
18-month job-seeker permit
Language tests
IELTS / TOEFL / German
Currency
EUR
Study · Germany

National (D) visa for study

For admitted students at a German university or preparatory course.

Key requirements

  • Admission letter from a German university
  • Blocked account (Sperrkonto) at the official annual amount
  • Health insurance for students
  • Academic certificates and language proof
  • Motivation letter and CV
Good to knowAfter graduation you can apply for a residence permit to look for work.
Typical processing
Depends on appointment availability

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · Germany

EU Blue Card, Skilled Worker & Opportunity Card

Work routes for graduates and qualified professionals, plus the points-based Opportunity Card for job search.

Key requirements

  • Recognised university degree or vocational qualification
  • Job offer meeting the salary threshold (Blue Card)
  • Recognition of qualifications (anabin / ZAB)
  • Opportunity Card points: qualification, language, experience, age
  • Health insurance and proof of funds
Good to knowBlue Card holders can qualify for permanent settlement faster with German skills.
Typical processing
Depends on appointment availability

How we help

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork

Tourist · Germany

Schengen visa (Type C)

Short stays of up to 90 days in any 180 days across the Schengen area.

Key requirements

  • Completed application and appointment
  • Travel medical insurance with at least €30,000 cover
  • Bank statements and income evidence
  • Flight and hotel reservations
  • Employment / business ties to Bangladesh
Good to knowApply to the country of your main destination.
Typical processing
About 15 days after appointment

How we help

  • Document checklist built around your employment and travel history
  • Bank statement, tax and employment evidence review
  • Cover letter and itinerary drafting
  • Online form completion and appointment booking

Family · Germany

Family reunification visa

For spouses and children joining a family member living in Germany.

Key requirements

  • Marriage or birth certificate, legalised
  • Basic German (A1) for spouses, with exceptions
  • Sponsor's residence permit, income and accommodation
  • Health insurance
  • Passport photos and application forms
Good to knowSpouses of Blue Card holders are exempt from the German language requirement.
Typical processing
Often several months

How we help

  • Route selection: spouse, partner, child, parent or dependant
  • Sponsor income and accommodation requirement review
  • Relationship evidence organisation and indexing
  • Certificate attestation and translation guidance

Medical · Germany

Visa for medical treatment

Schengen or national visa depending on the length of treatment.

Key requirements

  • Confirmation of appointment from the German hospital or doctor
  • Treatment cost estimate and advance payment or guarantee
  • Medical reports from Bangladesh
  • Proof of funds for stay
  • Travel insurance
Good to knowTreatments longer than 90 days require a national (D) visa.
Typical processing
Depends on appointment availability

How we help

  • Hospital and specialist shortlisting based on your diagnosis
  • Medical report sharing and treatment-plan / cost estimate requests
  • Hospital invitation letter follow-up
  • Patient and attendant visa applications filed together
