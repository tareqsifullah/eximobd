Study · India

Student visa

For full-time study at a recognised Indian institution.

Key requirements

  • Admission letter
  • Proof of funds
  • Academic documents
  • Online application and appointment at IVAC
  • Passport and photos
Good to knowStudy in India (SII) programme offers fee waivers.
Typical processing
About 1–3 weeks

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · India

Employment visa

For skilled professionals hired by Indian companies.

Key requirements

  • Employment contract
  • Salary above the minimum threshold
  • Company registration documents
  • Qualifications
  • Online application
Good to knowFRRO registration may be needed after arrival.
Typical processing
About 2–4 weeks

How we help

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork

Tourist · India

Tourist visa

Tourism and visiting friends and family.

Key requirements

  • Online application and IVAC appointment
  • Bank statement
  • Employment or student proof
  • Utility bill / NID
  • Passport and photo
Good to knowAppointment slots fill quickly — book early.
Typical processing
About 1–2 weeks

How we help

  • Document checklist built around your employment and travel history
  • Bank statement, tax and employment evidence review
  • Cover letter and itinerary drafting
  • Online form completion and appointment booking

Medical · India

Medical & Medical Attendant visas

For patients and up to two attendants.

Key requirements

  • Hospital appointment / invitation letter
  • Medical reports from Bangladesh
  • Bank statement
  • Passport and NID
  • Attendant applications linked to the patient
Good to knowMedical visas get priority appointments at IVAC.
Typical processing
Often within a week

How we help

  • Hospital and specialist shortlisting based on your diagnosis
  • Medical report sharing and treatment-plan / cost estimate requests
  • Hospital invitation letter follow-up
  • Patient and attendant visa applications filed together
