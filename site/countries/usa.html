United States

The US offers the widest choice of programmes and the most assistantships and funding at graduate level. Interview preparation at the US Embassy in Dhaka is a big part of the process.

Study · United States

F-1 student visa

For full-time academic study at a SEVP-certified school.

Key requirements

  • Form I-20 issued by the school
  • SEVIS I-901 fee payment
  • DS-160 form and visa appointment
  • Financial documents covering the first year (or full programme)
  • Interview at the US Embassy in Dhaka
Good to knowF-1 students may qualify for OPT, with an extension for eligible STEM degrees.
Typical processing
Depends on embassy appointment wait times

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · United States

H-1B, L-1 and O-1 visas

Employer-petitioned routes for specialty occupations, intra-company transferees and individuals with extraordinary ability.

Key requirements

  • US employer files a petition with USCIS
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent in a related field (H-1B)
  • Selection in the annual H-1B registration where applicable
  • DS-160 and consular interview after petition approval
  • Employment-based green card options for some profiles
Good to knowWe advise on eligibility and document preparation; petitions are filed by the US employer.
Typical processing
Varies by category and petition timeline

How we help

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork

Tourist · United States

B-1/B-2 visitor visa

Tourism, visiting relatives, and short business trips.

Key requirements

  • DS-160 form and visa fee payment
  • Evidence of strong ties to Bangladesh
  • Financial capacity for the trip
  • Travel purpose and itinerary
  • Interview at the US Embassy in Dhaka
Good to knowInterview wait times can be long — plan applications well ahead.
Typical processing
Depends on embassy appointment wait times

How we help

  • Document checklist built around your employment and travel history
  • Bank statement, tax and employment evidence review
  • Cover letter and itinerary drafting
  • Online form completion and appointment booking

Family · United States

Immigrant visas & F-2 dependants

Family-based green cards for relatives of US citizens and residents, and F-2 visas for student dependants.

Key requirements

  • Approved I-130 petition from the US relative
  • National Visa Center (NVC) document submission
  • Affidavit of Support (I-864) from the sponsor
  • Medical exam by an embassy-approved panel physician
  • Civil documents: birth, marriage and police certificates
Good to knowWaiting times depend on the family category and the Visa Bulletin.
Typical processing
Months to years, by category

How we help

  • Route selection: spouse, partner, child, parent or dependant
  • Sponsor income and accommodation requirement review
  • Relationship evidence organisation and indexing
  • Certificate attestation and translation guidance
Start my application → Compare family visas
Medical · United States

B-2 visa for medical treatment

Travel to the US for medical treatment, with attendants on B-2 visas.

Key requirements

  • Diagnosis from a local physician
  • Letter from the US doctor or hospital with treatment plan
  • Cost estimate for treatment and stay
  • Proof of funds or payment arrangements
  • Interview at the US Embassy in Dhaka
Good to knowEmergency appointment requests may be possible for urgent medical cases.
Typical processing
Depends on appointment availability

How we help

  • Hospital and specialist shortlisting based on your diagnosis
  • Medical report sharing and treatment-plan / cost estimate requests
  • Hospital invitation letter follow-up
  • Patient and attendant visa applications filed together
