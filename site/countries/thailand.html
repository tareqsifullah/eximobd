Study · Thailand

Non-Immigrant ED visa

For study at Thai universities and schools.

Key requirements

  • Acceptance letter
  • Institution's registration documents
  • Proof of funds
  • Passport and photos
  • Online e-Visa application
Good to knowExtensions are done in Thailand.
Typical processing
About 2 weeks

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · Thailand

Non-Immigrant B & LTR visas

For employment in Thailand, plus Long-Term Resident visas for skilled professionals.

Key requirements

  • Employment contract
  • Company documents
  • Work permit application
  • Degree certificates
  • Online e-Visa application
Good to knowA work permit is required in addition to the visa.
Typical processing
About 2–4 weeks

How we help

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork

Tourist · Thailand

Tourist visa (e-Visa)

Tourism stays, applied online.

Key requirements

  • Passport and photo
  • Bank statement
  • Return ticket
  • Hotel bookings
  • Employment proof
Good to knowApply through the official Thai e-Visa portal.
Typical processing
About 1–2 weeks

How we help

Family · Thailand

Non-Immigrant O visa

For family members of Thai nationals and dependants of work-visa holders.

Key requirements

  • Marriage / birth certificates
  • Sponsor's documents
  • Proof of funds
  • Passport and photos
  • Online application
Good to knowDocuments may need legalisation.
Typical processing
About 2–4 weeks

How we help

  • Route selection: spouse, partner, child, parent or dependant
  • Sponsor income and accommodation requirement review
  • Relationship evidence organisation and indexing
  • Certificate attestation and translation guidance

Medical · Thailand

Medical treatment visa

For patients receiving treatment at Thai hospitals, with attendants.

Key requirements

  • Hospital appointment letter
  • Medical reports
  • Proof of funds
  • Passport and photos
  • Attendant documents
Good to knowHospital international departments help with letters and pickup.
Typical processing
About 1–2 weeks

How we help

  • Hospital and specialist shortlisting based on your diagnosis
  • Medical report sharing and treatment-plan / cost estimate requests
  • Hospital invitation letter follow-up
  • Patient and attendant visa applications filed together
