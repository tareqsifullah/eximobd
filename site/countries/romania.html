Romania

Romania issues work permits to non-EU workers each year and joined the Schengen area in 2025. Choose employers carefully — leaving for another EU country without authorisation puts your status at risk.

Study · Romania

Study visa

For degree programmes at Romanian universities, including English-taught medicine.

Key requirements

  • Letter of acceptance from the Ministry of Education
  • Tuition payment
  • Proof of funds
  • Medical insurance
Good to knowMedical and dental degrees are popular and lower-cost.
Typical processing
Several weeks

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · Romania

Work permit & long-stay work visa (D/AM)

Employer obtains a work permit; you then apply for a long-stay employment visa.

Key requirements

  • Work permit issued to the employer by the General Inspectorate for Immigration
  • Long-stay employment visa (D/AM) application with the permit
  • Criminal record and medical certificates
  • Accommodation proof
  • Single residence permit after arrival
Good to knowWe review contracts and employer records before you pay any agency fee.
Typical processing
About 1–3 months

How we help

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork

Tourist · Romania

Schengen visa (Type C)

Short stays of up to 90 days in any 180 days.

Key requirements

  • Travel insurance
  • Bank statements
  • Bookings
  • Ties to Bangladesh
Good to knowRomania joined Schengen fully in January 2025.
Typical processing
About 15 days

How we help

  • Document checklist built around your employment and travel history
  • Bank statement, tax and employment evidence review
  • Cover letter and itinerary drafting
  • Online form completion and appointment booking

Family reunification visa

For spouses and children of residents.
