Study · Qatar

Student residence permit

For admitted students at Qatari universities, including Education City branch campuses.

Key requirements

  • Admission letter
  • University sponsorship
  • Medical and fingerprinting after arrival
Good to knowQatar University and HBKU offer merit scholarships.
Typical processing
Several weeks

How we help

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution

Work · Qatar

Work visa & residence permit (QID)

Employer-sponsored work visa completed at the Qatar Visa Center (QVC) in Dhaka.

Key requirements

  • Work visa issued to a Qatari employer
  • QVC Dhaka appointment: biometrics, medical and e-contract signing
  • Contract available in Bangla before you sign
  • BMET clearance and pre-departure orientation
  • Residence permit (QID) issued after arrival
Good to knowQatar's labour reforms set a minimum wage and allow job changes without the employer's permission, subject to notice rules.
Typical processing
About 2–4 weeks after QVC

How we help

  • Agency licence, employer and visa verification before you pay
  • Contract review — salary, hours, housing and return ticket
  • Medical, skills test and BMET clearance guidance
  • Language and skills course referrals to raise your wage

Tourist · Qatar

Tourist visa (Hayya)

Applied online through the Hayya platform.

Key requirements

  • Online Hayya application
  • Confirmed hotel booking
  • Return ticket
  • Passport valid for six months
Good to knowEligibility for Bangladeshi passports changes — we check before you book.
Typical processing
Usually a few days

How we help

  • Document checklist built around your employment and travel history
  • Bank statement, tax and employment evidence review
  • Cover letter and itinerary drafting
  • Online form completion and appointment booking

Family · Qatar

Family residence visa

Residents can sponsor a spouse and children.

Key requirements

  • Sponsor salary at the threshold
  • Attested marriage and birth certificates
  • Family accommodation
  • Medical examination
Good to knowCertificates need attestation in Bangladesh and by the Qatari embassy.
Typical processing
Several weeks

How we help

  • Route selection: spouse, partner, child, parent or dependant
  • Sponsor income and accommodation requirement review
  • Relationship evidence organisation and indexing
  • Certificate attestation and translation guidance
