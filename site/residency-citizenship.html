Residency and a second passport.

For families and business owners who want wider travel access, a second home or a Plan B. We work in partnership with licensed lawyers and government-authorised agents in each programme country, who file your application with the government.

03For Bangladeshi nationals

Four things to get right first.

Dual citizenship

Bangladesh permits dual citizenship with 101 countries, including North America, Europe and 12 Caribbean states, but not SAARC countries. You need a dual-nationality certificate, and dual citizens cannot stand for parliament. We check your chosen programme against the current list.

Source and transfer of funds

Investment funds must be legally earned and documented. Bangladesh Bank foreign-exchange rules restrict moving capital abroad, so most clients invest with funds earned or held outside Bangladesh. Your lawyer and banks will ask for full proof.

Due diligence

Every programme runs background checks on all adult applicants. Governments can refuse, and have revoked citizenship obtained with false information. Full, honest disclosure is essential.

Programme risk

The EU has pressed Caribbean states over their programmes, and visa-free access can change. We explain the current risks of each programme so you can choose with care.

04With our lawyer partners

How the process works.

Eximo is your adviser and coordinator in Bangladesh. The licensed lawyer or authorised agent in the programme country is your legal representative and files with the government.

  1. Confidential review

    Family, goals, budget and source of funds — under a confidentiality agreement.

  2. Programme choice

    A written comparison of suitable programmes, costs and risks.

  3. Partner lawyer engaged

    You sign directly with a licensed lawyer or authorised agent. We introduce and coordinate.

  4. Documents from Bangladesh

    Police clearance, birth and marriage certificates, attestation, translation and source-of-funds evidence.

  5. Government due diligence

    The application is filed and checked; you are kept informed at each stage.

  6. Approval & investment

    Funds go to the government or approved project only after approval in principle; then the certificate and passport are issued.

05Our standards

What we will never do.

  • Sell or “guarantee” a passport. Citizenship is granted only by the issuing government.
  • Take investment or government fees into Eximo or personal accounts. These are paid to government or escrow accounts.
  • Help hide the source of funds or anyone’s background.
  • Recommend a programme without explaining its risks in writing.
