Career counselling

Choose the course for the career.

Before you choose a country, decide where you want to be in five years. We help students and professionals choose subjects, routes and destinations that lead to real jobs.

For students

After HSC, A-levels or a bachelor’s

  • Subject and course choice matched to employment outcomes
  • Country comparison by cost, work rights and job market
  • Funding plan: scholarships, part-time work and family budget
For professionals

Mid-career moves abroad

  • Skilled-migration eligibility and points
  • CV rewritten for the target market
  • When a master’s abroad strengthens your case, and when it doesn’t

From questions to a written plan.

  1. Discovery call

    Where you are now and what you want.

  2. Profile review

    Academics, experience, finances and constraints.

  3. Options report

    Two or three realistic routes with costs and risks.

  4. Action plan

    Tests, documents, applications and deadlines.

  5. Follow-up

    Check-ins as you move through applications.
