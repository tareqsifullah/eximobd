Overseas employment

Work abroad, safely.

More than a million Bangladeshis took jobs overseas in 2025. We help you pick the right market, check that the offer is real, and prepare for departure — working only with BMET-licensed recruiting agencies.

02Routes

Three ways to work abroad.

Government channels

Government-to-government recruitment with set costs and official selection.

  • South Korea — EPS via BOESL
  • Japan — Specified Skilled Worker
  • Malaysia — approved channel

Employer recruitment

An overseas employer issues a visa through a BMET-licensed agency. This is how most Gulf jobs work.

03Before you fly

The departure process.

Every worker leaving Bangladesh legally goes through these steps. We walk you through each one and check your documents along the way.

  1. Verify the job

    Check the recruiting agency's BMET licence and confirm the visa and employer on the destination country's official portal.

  2. Medical & skills

    Medical at an approved centre (GAMCA for the Gulf), and any required skills test — SVP for Saudi Arabia, BCA certificate for Singapore, EPS-TOPIK for Korea.

  3. Contract

    Read and sign the contract before you travel. Salary, hours, food, accommodation and return ticket should all be written down.

  4. BMET clearance

    Registration, pre-departure orientation and the emigration smart card. Your welfare-board membership gives insurance and support abroad.

  5. Travel & register

    Register with your embassy or labour wing after arrival and keep copies of every document.

04Protect yourself

How to spot recruitment fraud.

  • The agency cannot show a valid BMET recruiting licence.
  • You are asked to pay into a personal bank or mobile-money account.
  • The visa cannot be verified on the destination country’s official portal.
  • You are promised a “free visa” to work for anyone — this is illegal in the Gulf.
  • Someone offers to sell a Korean EPS job, an Italian nulla osta or a Japanese CoE. These are not for sale.
  • You are not given a written contract before departure.

05Prepare

Skills and language open better jobs.

Japan’s SSW needs JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic, Korea’s EPS needs EPS-TOPIK, and Italy’s long-term permit needs Italian A2. We have language partnerships for Japanese, Korean, Italian, German and IELTS.
