Digital nomad visas

Work remotely, live abroad.

For Bangladeshi freelancers, remote employees and online business owners earning from clients outside the host country. These visas let you live abroad legally while you keep working for them.

02For Bangladeshi applicants

What makes a strong file.

Show your income clearly

Bank statements showing foreign income received through legal channels, plus contracts or platform statements (Upwork, Fiverr, employer payslips). Income received outside banking channels is hard to prove.

Prove the work is remote

An employer letter confirming remote work, or client contracts showing your clients are outside the host country.

Check consular jurisdiction

Some European consulates handle Bangladeshi applications from New Delhi or another hub. We confirm where to apply and how long appointments take.

Plan for tax and insurance

Health insurance is mandatory almost everywhere. Long stays can create tax residence, so we explain the basics and refer you to a tax adviser where needed.
