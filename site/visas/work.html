Work visas

Work visas

We assess which work route you actually qualify for — employer-sponsored, points-based or job-seeker — and prepare a file that matches the rules of that route, not a generic checklist.

01What we handle

Your file, done properly.

  • Eligibility check against each country's skilled-occupation lists
  • Points calculation and skills-assessment guidance
  • CV and job-search positioning for the target market
  • Review of employer offers and sponsorship paperwork
  • Visa and dependant applications
  • Pathway planning toward permanent residence where available
02The process

Step by step.

  1. Eligibility & points check

  2. Skills assessment

  3. Job offer / nomination

  4. Visa application

  5. Arrival & settlement
