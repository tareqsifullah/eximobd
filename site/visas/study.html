Study visas

Student visas

From shortlisting universities to the visa interview, we handle the whole study-abroad file — with honest advice on cost, intake timing and what you can realistically get admitted to.

01What we handle

Your file, done properly.

  • Course and university shortlist matched to your grades, budget and career goal
  • Scholarship and tuition-waiver search, with deadlines tracked for you
  • SOP, CV and reference-letter review
  • Offer letter, CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA follow-up with the institution
  • Proof-of-funds planning and bank document checks
  • Visa file preparation and mock interview
02The process

Step by step.

  1. Profile assessment

  2. Shortlist & apply

  3. Offer & funding

  4. Visa file & interview

  5. Pre-departure briefing
