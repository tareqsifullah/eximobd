Medical visas

Medical treatment visas

When treatment is urgent, paperwork should not slow you down. We coordinate with hospitals for invitation letters and cost estimates, and prepare visas for the patient and attendants together.

Last reviewed: November 2026

01What we handle

Your file, done properly.

  • Hospital and specialist shortlisting based on your diagnosis
  • Medical report sharing and treatment-plan / cost estimate requests
  • Hospital invitation letter follow-up
  • Patient and attendant visa applications filed together
  • Funds and payment-evidence preparation
  • Urgent / expedited appointment guidance where available
02The process

Step by step.

  1. Medical records review

  2. Hospital & estimate

  3. Invitation letter

  4. Patient & attendant visas

  5. Travel & follow-up
