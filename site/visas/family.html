Family visas

Family & dependant visas

Family visas hinge on proving a genuine relationship and meeting the sponsor's financial threshold. We organise the evidence so the relationship is easy for an officer to see.

01What we handle

Your file, done properly.

  • Route selection: spouse, partner, child, parent or dependant
  • Sponsor income and accommodation requirement review
  • Relationship evidence organisation and indexing
  • Certificate attestation and translation guidance
  • Application forms, fees and biometrics booking
  • Coordination between sponsor abroad and applicant in Bangladesh
02The process

Step by step.

  1. Route & sponsor check

  2. Evidence gathering

  3. Attestation & translation

  4. Application

  5. Decision & joining
