07Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need IELTS to study abroad?

Not always. Some UK, Malaysian and European universities accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter or their own English test. Visa rules may still require a test, so we check both the university and the visa requirement for your case.

How much money do I need to show for a student visa?

It depends on the country, city and course length. We calculate the exact figure for your offer — tuition plus the official living-cost amount — and plan how and when the funds should be held.

Can you guarantee a visa?

No honest consultancy can. Decisions are made only by the embassy or immigration authority. What we can do is make sure your file is complete, consistent and matches the rules — which is where most refusals start.

I had a visa refusal before. Can I apply again?

Usually, yes. We read the refusal letter, fix the reasons given and decide whether to reapply now or strengthen the profile first.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We search university scholarships, tuition waivers and government awards such as Chevening, Commonwealth, DAAD, MEXT, GKS and Australia Awards, and help with the application essays.

How do I know an overseas job offer is genuine?

Check that the recruiting agency holds a valid BMET licence, verify the visa and employer on the destination country's official portal, and never pay into a personal account. Send us the documents and we will check them before you pay anything.

Can Eximo help me get a second passport?

We advise on lawful citizenship- and residency-by-investment programmes and work with licensed lawyers and government-authorised agents in each programme country, who file the application. Funds must be legally sourced and moved in line with Bangladesh Bank foreign-exchange rules.

What does a consultation cost?

The first consultation is free. After that, we explain service fees in writing before you commit to anything.
