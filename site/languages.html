Language courses

The language comes first.

Scores decide admissions, work visas and migration points. We match you with a verified partner institute in person or online, then track your progress to the exam and into your application.

02Verified partners

How we choose our institutes.

We recommend a provider only after it meets every standard below, and we review partners each term.

Registered and accountable

A legally registered institute with a physical address, published fees and a written refund policy.

Qualified teachers

Teachers with recognised qualifications or certified exam experience in the language they teach.

Results we can see

Exam results are shared with us, so we can see real pass rates and score gains, not marketing claims.

Student feedback

We collect feedback from every Eximo student each term and drop partners who fall short.

Our language partnershipsWe have partnerships with established institutes for Japanese, Italian, IELTS, German and Korean. Your course plan names the institute, its location and timetable before you enrol.
03Enrolment

From placement to exam day.

  1. Placement test

    A short assessment to find your current level.

  2. Course plan

    Target score, partner institute, schedule and fees — in writing.

  3. Classes

    In person in person or online, with progress reports shared with your advisor.

  4. Mock exams

    Timed practice tests under exam conditions.

  5. Exam & application

    We book the official exam and add your result to your visa or admission file.
