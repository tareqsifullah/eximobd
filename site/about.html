About Eximo

Advice you can decide on.

Eximo helps students, workers and professionals choose the right destination, programme and visa route, and prepares applications that meet the rules the first time.

What we believe

Good advice sometimes means “not yet”.

Moving abroad is one of the largest financial decisions a family makes. We would rather tell you to improve your IELTS score, wait an intake or walk away from a doubtful job offer than submit an application we don’t believe in.

Honest

Clear eligibility assessments and written fees before you commit.

Thorough

Every document checked against current rules.

Personal

One advisor from start to finish.

Partnered

Verified language institutes and licensed lawyers abroad.
